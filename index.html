Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

Seeking Greens collects golf round data you enter manually, including hole scores, shot distances, and shot types. We also store your display name, region, and app preferences.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to calculate and display your personal golf analytics. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data in any way.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore and is associated with your account. Only you can access your round data. If you opt into the leaderboard, aggregate performance metrics are visible to other users.

Authentication

We use Firebase Authentication to manage accounts. You may sign in anonymously or with an email address. No passwords are stored by us directly.

Data Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Deleting the app does not remove your data from our servers.

Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact us at seekinggreensapp@gmail.com.
